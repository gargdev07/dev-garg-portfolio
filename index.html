01 — DATA SYSTEMSRELATIONAL MODEL / DASHBOARD

PropAnalytics

Where does a property market start to show a pattern? PropAnalytics gives 38,095+ listings a structure that supports both analysis and a usable dashboard.

Question Organize listings across four cities into a reliable relational model while keeping analytical queries responsive.

Approach Structured 38,095+ listings into a third-normal-form schema across eight tables, with composite indexes and foreign-key constraints. Three materialized views support analytical queries.

Built A FastAPI REST backend with JWT authentication and 15+ CRUD endpoints, connected to a live dashboard. The dashboard tracked ₹3,186 Cr in sales value.

PostgreSQLFastAPIPythonNext.jsJWTMaterialized Views

TAKEAWAY Data modeling and product interfaces work best when planned together.

02 — FINANCIAL DATATIME SERIES / 44 YEARS

AAPL: analysis
& forecasting

What patterns hold across decades of Apple data, and where does uncertainty begin? This study follows the historical series, tests a strategy, and forecasts with uncertainty visible.

Analyze Studied 44 years of historical data (1980–2024) across more than 11,000 rows. Engineered 20-, 50-, and 200-day moving averages, rolling volatility, and daily returns to explore market cycles and seasonal patterns.

Backtest Compared a Golden Cross strategy against the historical series. The project CV reports a $10K → $1.2M+ historical backtest result; it is not an actual investment outcome or a promise of future returns.

Forecast Built a Meta Prophet model for 365-day trend estimates with confidence intervals. Forecasts are uncertain and are not financial advice.

PythonPandasMeta ProphetMatplotlibBacktesting

TAKEAWAY Separate what history says from what a model estimates next.

03 — PROBABILITYSTOCHASTIC SYSTEM

Ball-by-ball IPL simulator

What changes when a match moves from one state to another? The simulator uses probability to explore how conditions and strategy can shift possible T20 outcomes.

Model Represented T20 outcomes with a seven-state Markov Chain and phase-specific transition matrices.

Simulate & compare Explored Aggressive, Conservative, and Balanced strategies across stochastic match simulations.

Inspect Built an interactive UI for score distributions, win curves, and over-by-over momentum. Validation against real IPL data is reported in the CV as an average of approximately 200 runs across 6–10 wickets.

JavaScriptMarkov ChainsStochastic ProcessesStatistical Modelling

TAKEAWAY Probabilistic models can make uncertainty explorable.
